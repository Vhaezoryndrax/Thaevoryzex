# Thaevoryzex

> The snow contained a warm egg. The nest remembered a creature made of summer.

## The Court beneath the Frozen Lake

Thaevoryzex is visible through a lake whose surface never thaws. Beneath the ice lies a court of pale pillars, each surrounded by snow that falls upward toward the frozen ceiling.

Travelers reach the court by placing an empty cup upon the lake. If the cup fills with frost, they remain above. If it stays empty, a staircase appears in its reflection, and the traveler finds the first step beneath a foot that has not moved.

The court contains a throne shaped like a nest. Upon it rests a dark egg, warm enough to melt the snow before the flakes can touch its surface. Its shell shows the reflection of a green valley, where a single tree casts the shadow of folded wings.

## The Judge without a Season

The court's judge wears a robe of white feathers over a body covered in dark scales. Nobody sees the judge arrive. A traveler notices the robe upon the throne, then notices that the egg has moved into the judge's hands.

Every hearing begins with the same question: which season did you leave outside the cup?

Some travelers answer winter. Others answer summer or the name of a month. The judge accepts none of these replies. Instead, one pillar becomes transparent, revealing a small room where the traveler stands beside a cup filled with green leaves.

The hearing continues until that reflected traveler drinks. Nobody remembers the taste. Everyone remembers the room growing warmer around a window that begins to show falling snow.

## The Petition Written upon a Feather

A petitioner arrived with a feather sealed inside glass. Its color changed whenever he attempted to describe it. He asked the judge to identify the creature that had lost it.

The judge placed the egg against the glass. Inside the reflected valley, the tree's shadow unfolded into a pale dragon. It turned its head toward the petitioner and opened a mouth filled with falling snow.

The petitioner dropped the glass. It did not break. Instead, it sank into the floor, leaving the feather suspended where his hands had been.

One pillar acquired his name. Another acquired the outline of a window. The judge asked whether he still wished to know which creature had lost the feather.

The petitioner answered that he now wished to know which room had lost its season. The judge closed the hearing without returning his cup.

## The Testimony of the Seven Cups

Seven cups stand beneath the throne. Six contain frost. The last contains a small dark circle that reflects the egg from a place inside its shell.

The court attendants keep the cups covered with cloth. They remove the covers only when the upward snow stops falling. During those pauses, the dark circle becomes bright enough to show a nest inside a green tree.

An attendant placed a finger against the circle. He felt the warmth of feathers and heard someone asking the judge's opening question. The voice was his own, speaking from behind a window filled with snow.

He withdrew his hand. Frost formed upon one fingernail and remained throughout the year. Whenever it melted, the seventh cup became empty, and the judge's robe could be heard moving in the unoccupied throne.

## The Words beneath the Shell

**Thae — vor — yzex — aluun.**

These sounds appear as faint marks upon the egg when its reflected valley enters evening. The judge turns the shell slowly, allowing each mark to disappear before the next becomes visible.

A scribe attempted to preserve them on a feather. His writing described the court, the cups, and the petitioner holding a glass case. It did not contain the sounds he believed he had copied.

He began again. This time, the feather showed the green valley with a small frozen lake beneath its tree. Through that lake, a judge could be seen holding an egg whose shell reflected the scribe.

The scribe placed the feather in the seventh cup. The dark circle covered his writing, leaving one narrow mark resembling a closed wing.

## A Verdict Heard through Ice

> Who warms the egg beneath the lake?
>
> The season whose window has not opened.
>
> Who lost the feather inside the glass?
>
> The creature that mistook the court for its nest.
>
> When will the hearing end?
>
> When the cup remembers which side held the snow.

These replies were heard above the lake by travelers unable to enter. They pressed their ears against the ice and found the judge's voice moving beneath them, though the court remained still.

One traveler heard a fourth reply. He carried it home inside an empty cup. By spring, the cup held a green leaf whose shadow resembled a dark egg.

## The Warmth outside the Verdict

The court continues receiving petitioners beneath its frozen ceiling. Its judge holds the shell through hearings that last longer than the travelers can count.

The upward snow occasionally pauses. The seventh cup remains covered during those moments, and the attendants listen for feathers moving upon the throne.

Thaevoryzex waits inside the valley reflected upon the egg. Above the lake, a traveler may find the first stair beneath an unmoving foot and discover that the summer left outside his cup has begun to hatch somewhere below.
